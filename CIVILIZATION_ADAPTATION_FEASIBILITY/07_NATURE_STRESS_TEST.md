# 07 — Nature-level stress test

| criterion | verdict | basis |
|---|---|---|
| A. Conceptual novelty | WEAK-MODERATE | Rigidity-trap/lock-in mechanism is 15–25 years old. Novelty = first large-N empirical test + sign-reversal prediction, not a new principle. Must be framed as empirical test of an existing concept. |
| B. Empirical generality | MODERATE | Crops (strong), settlement (partial), historical crises (narrow-N, biased), livestock (rejected). Two domains can genuinely carry the mechanism — just enough for "general" if executed well. |
| C. Scale | MODERATE | Contemporary: ~160 countries / global grids — good. Historical: ~21 NGAs with usable env × legacy — small and clustered; cross-civilizational claim rests on ~6 world regions. |
| D. Identification | WEAK | Wealth/state-capacity/geography confounds severe; exposure-frame redesign is mandatory; no natural experiment identified yet. As designed, β3 is descriptive. |
| E. Measurement validity | MODERATE | Crop concentration (Herfindahl) and GAEZ-based counterfactuals are defensible; "legacy dependence" as a composite would be arbitrary — separate dimensions required. CrisisDB outcomes ≠ transformation — the biggest validity gap. |
| F. Falsifiability | GOOD | Interaction sign, T* existence, and sign-reversal are all falsifiable; falsification plan exists (05). |
| G. Cross-domain coherence | WEAK-MODERATE | Do CrisisDB complexity, modern crop concentration, and GAEZ suitability measure ONE mechanism? Plausible but unproven — the paper must argue one mechanism, not collect three analogous patterns. |
| H. Robustness | WEAK | Pilot: interaction sign flips under alternate legacy measure (historical) and is opposite-signed (contemporary). Current evidence does not survive reasonable alternative specs. |
| I. Visual/explanatory power | GOOD | 4–5 figure arc exists: mechanism schematic → historical interaction → global retention-burden map → T* map → falsification panel. |
| J. Scientific consequence | MODERATE-GOOD | If the interaction is real and robust, it reframes adaptation capacity as non-monotone in accumulated capital — a genuine consequence for adaptation economics and managed retreat. |

Overall: the *concept* can reach Nature level only if the weak criteria (D, E, H) are
resolved by redesign — exposure frame, direct transformation outcomes, preregistered
legacy dimensions, and a result that survives the multiverse. Today's evidence base does
not yet show the hypothesized sign; the study must be run as a genuine test, not a
confirmation exercise.
