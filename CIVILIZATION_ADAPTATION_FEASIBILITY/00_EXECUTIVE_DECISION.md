DECISION: CONDITIONAL GO

## 1. One-sentence reason
The environment × legacy interaction is estimable from fully public data in two domains
(historical crises, contemporary countries) and the crop counterfactual layer is computable,
but the central outcome — *transformational* flexibility — is not directly observable in
public historical data, and pilot interactions are directionally unstable, so the paper is
viable only after a specific redesign and only under a weaker framing than "civilizational
sign reversal".

## 2. Strongest empirical path
Agriculture: MapSPAM/FAOSTAT crop-area concentration (Herfindahl staple dependence) ×
climate exposure → crop switching/diversification, plus a GAEZ-based
V_retain(T) vs V_substitute(T) map of T*. Both counterfactual states are public model
outputs; no invented parameters.

## 3. Weakest component
The historical transformation outcome. CrisisDB codes decline/collapse/conflict — not
transformation/relocation/restructuring. No public event-level dataset codes societal
transformation. The layer must either proxy transformation via radiocarbon/HYDE
redistribution dynamics or accept the narrower outcome of persistence-failure.

## 4. Most serious identification threat
Censoring-by-measurement in the historical layer: literate complex states are both more
recorded and higher legacy, truncating the legacy range exactly where the hypothesis is
load-bearing; second threat is exposure-frame conditioning (crisis samples are selected
on having crises, not on climate exposure).

## 5. Historical layer viable?
Conditionally — demonstrated join (99% NGA mapping, 98% legacy join, 62% env join; usable
N≈72–101 crises over ~21–30 NGAs) but only with an exposure-frame redesign and acceptance
of a narrower outcome; the African coverage gap in PAGES 2k must be closed (LMR/products).

## 6. Contemporary layer viable?
Yes for crops and settlement-persistence proxies; country-level reallocation pilot works
(N=162) but shows an opposite-signed interaction — hypothesis not currently supported
there. Livestock/food-utilization is NOT viable as an empirical layer.

## 7. Future-counterfactual layer viable?
Yes for crops (GAEZ + MapSPAM/FAOSTAT; T* computable as sensitivity-banded parameter,
model-dependence must be reported across ≥2 GCMs). Partial for settlement (needs authored
destination rule). No for livestock.

## 8. Is "legacy dependence" measurable?
Yes, as 2–3 separate preregistered dimensions (infrastructure intensity, settlement/land-use
persistence, staple concentration) — NOT as one weighted index.

## 9. Is "transformational flexibility" measurable?
Crop switching/diversification and population redistribution: yes, directly.
Institutional transformation post-crisis: no public data — the key gap.

## 10. Is a sign reversal of civilizational capital testable?
Not with current public data. The asset→liability reversal requires within-unit
transformation outcomes across a large env-mismatch gradient — unavailable.

## 11. Retention Burden estimable?
Empirically estimable ONLY for crops (GAEZ counterfactuals). Conceptual elsewhere unless
a settlement destination model is authored.

## 12. Estimated usable N
- Historical crises: ~72–101 events, ~21–30 NGAs effective.
- Contemporary countries: ~160 (crop domain could use ~180 or grid cells in millions).
- Future counterfactual: 173 countries (yield-based) or gridded cells (GAEZ).

## 13. Exact datasets recommended
- MapSPAM (gridded crop area) + FAOSTAT area/production panel → staple dependence + switching outcome.
- GAEZ v4 baseline + climate-change suitability → V_retain/V_substitute.
- GHSL epochal built surface + WorldPop → settlement persistence/redistribution.
- OWID/Berkeley-Earth country anomalies; ERA5/WorldClim for gridded work.
- CrisisDB + Equinox + LMR/TRENMay environmental products → historical secondary layer.
- Leaving Place, Restoring Home relocation cases → outcome validation.

## 14. Datasets rejected and why
- Livestock utilization domain: edibility/utilization fractions not observed globally (keep as discussion).
- eHRAF/SCCS: paywalled, N=186, snapshot → not usable as main layer.
- LandScan: proprietary licensing — use WorldPop instead.
- Composite "civilization score": arbitrary weighting — rejected by design.

## 15. Recommended Nature-level main claim
"Across agricultural and settlement systems, pre-existing legacy dependence interacts with
environmental change to determine whether societies transform or persist under strain" —
i.e. a general, cross-domain empirical test of adaptation lock-in (naming the rigidity-trap
lineage), with the asset→liability sign reversal posed as a secondary, falsifiable
prediction only where data support it (crops).

## 16. Claims that must NOT be made
- Any causal/civilizational sign-reversal claim ("prosperity becomes liability").
- "Sunk-cost paradox" as mechanism (sunk cost ≠ switching cost).
- Transformation claims from collapse/conflict outcomes alone.
- Any claim resting on a composite index with author-chosen weights.
- Universality ("civilization" level claims) from ~21 NGAs.

## 17. Recommended next phase
A preregistered crop-domain lead study: build staple-concentration × climate exposure
panel + GAEZ counterfactual T* map + falsification battery; keep the historical layer as a
secondary, exposure-frame-rebuilt analysis; drop livestock to discussion.
